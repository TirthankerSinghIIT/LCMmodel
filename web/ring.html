LCM One robot at a time · robots see only which nodes are occupied

Robots stand on the nodes of a ring. Each one sees which nodes are occupied, but not how many robots share a node, and has no sense of left or right. They take turns in a fixed order they do not know; on its turn a robot stays or steps to a neighbouring node. Every start gathers on one node within n − 3 rounds, except the few starts no algorithm can gather, which the page flags.

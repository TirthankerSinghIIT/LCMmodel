LCM Gathering on a ring GatheRRing · round robin · no multiplicity detection

Robots sit on the nodes of a ring and see only which nodes are occupied: never how many robots a node holds. They have no sense of direction. The round-robin scheduler activates them one at a time in a fixed order they do not know; an activated robot stays or steps to a neighbouring node. Algorithm GatheRRing (Navarra & Piselli, arXiv:2502.03939) gathers them from every start outside the unsolvable set, in at most n − 3 epochs. Click a node to add a robot; right-click (or long-press) to remove one.
